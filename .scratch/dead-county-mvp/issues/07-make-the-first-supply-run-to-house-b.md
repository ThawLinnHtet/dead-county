# 07: Make the first supply run to House B

**What to build:** Leave House A by either route, recognize House B from the first street junction, and collect its guaranteed food from the kitchen.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Fight with the bat and read the outcome

**Status:** done

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At default zoom, a newcomer can identify House B by gray siding and its distinctive porch and point out a plausible route without map assistance.
- [x] The direct route is shorter and more exposed. The detour is longer and uses solid geometry to break line of sight. Compare both with equivalent endpoints and ordinary zombie behavior.
- [x] Each route leads through the normal open entrance to a short, readable kitchen path with at least one obtainable Canned Food in the designated container on a fresh run.
- [ ] Under a viable encounter, the newcomer can enter, search the kitchen container, transfer the can and leave without a mandatory fight. A chasing zombie can use the same doorway and path.

At 1280 × 720 and default zoom, the junction view shows House B's gray siding and its porch and steps. Chrome keyboard runs collected and equipped the starter bat, took each route on a fresh run, transferred the kitchen can and left without attacking. The cautious detour included about three seconds of hesitation in House A.

With matched endpoints, walking took about 6.8 seconds on the direct route and 9.0 seconds on the detour. In the matched zombie encounter, the survivor spent about 5.5 seconds in sight on the direct route and 2.3 seconds on the detour. Coordinator checks also verified the yard fence blocks sight within detection range and pursuit can follow the survivor through the open entrance to the kitchen.

An unaided newcomer playtest still needs to confirm recognition from the junction and navigation to the kitchen during a viable encounter. Need-rate tuning and save restoration of the transferred can require their later integrations.
